# HSWM causal-composition research program

Status: **DESIGN_SEEDED / SCIENTIFICALLY_CONNECTED / INTEGRATED_CLAIM_UNJUDGED**.

## Purpose and authority boundary

This program fixes the order in which HSWM's local causal-learning and fractal
composition claims may be tested. It is a `SECONDARY_AI` research design grounded
in the canonical HSWM identity and the eight USER_PRIMARY fractal composition
laws. It does not add a ninth FCL law, change HSWM's target identity, or establish
that any present implementation is cognitive, conscious, self-like, efficacious,
or closed across scale.

The target is one token-native LLM-function macro-neural network whose evolving
canonical hypergraph is simultaneously living harness, world model, and
continuous learner. The research question is whether this target can be made
causally real rather than represented in documentation, a KG, a trace store, or
an agent workflow.

The decisive recurrence is:

```text
sealed typed trajectory
→ independently grounded outcome
→ counterfactual credit
→ owner-valid canonical revision
→ changed held-out next behavior
```

For composition, the same schema-level grammar must recur:

```text
compose(HSWM_1, ..., HSWM_n)
→ HSWM with Step / Learn / Inv / Permit / identity-learning lineage
```

The research program, its tests, and its KG projection are bounded inquiry
artifacts. They are not the cognition, routing, learning, or canonical state of
HSWM.

## Inheritance-first execution rule

The gate order is implemented under the
[`HSWM reuse-first research architecture`](../../docs/research/HSWM_REUSE_FIRST_ARCHITECTURE_2026-08-30.md).
G1 does not reimplement established memory, textual reflection, skill-library,

- `B1`: pinned RAG or Letta-style durable-memory baseline;

disposition, isolated state, matched-resource receipt, and named control family.

not evidence that its mechanism is HSWM cognition. HSWM directly implements only
the unresolved causal seam: outcome-credit-owner-`Permit` admission, canonical
revision identity, compiled behavioral mediation, and exact intervention.

The order is a partially ordered research graph rather than a rhetorical roadmap.
`G2a` and `G2b` may run in parallel after `G1`; they must both close before a
multiscale topology claim is admitted.

```text
G0 measurement integrity
  → G1 local causal rung                         FCL-1
     ├→ G2a counterfactual multiscale credit    FCL-4
```

A downstream gate cannot repair or reinterpret an upstream failure. A failed
gate narrows the target mechanism or returns work to the named predecessor; it
does not authorize post-hoc relabeling of a wrapper, retrieval system, or
orchestrator as HSWM.

## Gate contracts

### G0 — measurement integrity

G0 is a construct- and instrument-validity prerequisite, not an efficacy result.

- Freeze one bounded task family, held-out distribution, model and tool surface,
  resource accounting, outcome source, and intervention schedule.
- Before any outcome is inspected, bind sample size, seed count, stopping rule,
  effect margin, uncertainty and multiplicity methods, pilot-exclusion rules,
  task-family selection, analysis code, and the allowed holdout-inspection
  budget. Preserve an all-run manifest, including invalid and negative runs.
- Address canonical state, compiled state, readset, writeset, trajectory,
  outcome, credit, revision proposal, admission receipt, and later behavior
  separately.
- Seal the trajectory before outcome observation. Keep outcome production
  independent of the action producer and revision proposer.
- Make canonical revision remove/restore executable before the first efficacy
  run. Compiled state must be derivable from the identified canonical revision.
- Record task quality, calls, tokens, context, compute, wall time, human minutes,
  static instruction load, interpretation work, manual exceptions, invalid-run
  fraction, and unobserved-effect risk for every arm.

Pass only when matched-budget execution, outcome independence, leakage probes,
instrument calibration, and exact intervention identity are all demonstrable.
Otherwise the terminal is `INCONCLUSIVE_MEASUREMENT_NOT_READY`; do not add a more
elaborate judge or ontology as a substitute. Claim ceiling: `MEASUREMENT_READY`.

### G1 — minimal outcome-bound causal rung

G1 asks whether one bounded revision family realizes FCL-1.

- Hold the sealed trajectory and outcome fixed where the protocol permits.
- Compare admitted revision, no learning, log-only, RAG or text lesson,
  hand-authored static rule, inactive sham revision, wrong-target revision,
  shuffled outcome or credit, revision removal, and byte-identical restoration.
- Trace the full mediation path from revision through compiled disposition and
  action distribution to fresh held-out outcome.

Pass only if the preregistered fresh-task effect is present, removal eliminates
the effect, restoration recovers it, and sham or shuffled credit fails to
reproduce it under matched budgets. If log-only or static prompt edits explain
the gain, the result is retrieval or harness adaptation rather than HSWM causal
learning. Claim ceiling: `LOCAL_CAUSAL_REVISION_UNDER_DECLARED_TASK`.

### G2a — counterfactual multiscale credit

G2a addresses shared and delayed outcome before coalition explanations become
post-hoc stories.

- Address cell, role-bearing incidence, coalition, revision, and whole-level
  contributions separately.
- Intervene on one contribution while preserving the other information and
  action budget.
- Compare counterfactual credit with uniform, shuffled, global-only, forced
  one-to-one, and retrospective LLM-judge credit.
- Measure attribution calibration, duplicate-credit inflation, uncertainty,
  negative-credit false positives, removal sensitivity, and replay agreement.

Pass only if credit tracks controlled causal contributions and credit shuffling
destroys the G1 learning effect. A whole-system outcome without attributable
credit cannot unlock topology learning. Claim ceiling:
`BOUNDED_COUNTERFACTUAL_CREDIT`.

### G2b — dynamic role-bearing n-ary coalition

G2b asks whether situation-dependent coalition and higher-order incidence do
causal work beyond a fixed workflow.

- The task context may ignite membership, roles, and message paths; a top-level
  controller may transport compute but may not prescribe the semantic roster.
- Seal coalition membership and role incidence before outcome.
- Match model, tools, messages, and budget against fixed router, fixed roster,
  fixed role, information-matched computational oracle, centralized workflow,
  lossy and information-matched pairwise encodings, flat event table, and
  role-shuffled controls. A privileged-information oracle is an upper bound,
  not an arm in the causal ranking.

Pass only if the dynamic n-ary arm has a preregistered held-out advantage and
the advantage disappears under pairwise or role-shuffled intervention. Storing
a hyperedge is not evidence that it affected execution. Claim ceiling:
`BOUNDED_DYNAMIC_NARY_COORDINATION`.

### G3 — topology morphogenesis and damage recovery

G3 is stronger than episode-local activation. It requires outcome-bound durable
change to roles, incidences, interaction order, or topology.

- Admit add, remove, split, merge, specialize, separate, prune, and restore only
  through the same owner-valid revision plane used by G1.
- Apply task-regime shift, member loss, incidence failure, capability loss, and
  controlled lesion.
- Compare with fixed topology plus adaptive activation, parameter-only
  adaptation, random rewiring, degree/density-matched rewiring, inactive sham
  rewiring, and leakage-trained topology.

Pass only if learned topology improves fresh, retention, and recovery measures;
the causal topology intervention mediates the gain; and recovery occurs inside
the declared resource and time window. Unbounded growth, manual exception
repair, snapshot replacement, or central rerouting is a failure signal. Claim
ceiling: `BOUNDED_OUTCOME_LINKED_MORPHOGENESIS`.

### G4 — joint world-self model and diachronic continuity

G4 must close before composition because a composite cannot be distinguished
from federation without a measurable boundary and lineage.

- Represent external entities and events together with membership, role,
  capability, topology, reliability, uncertainty, permission boundary, and
  lineage in the same typed canonical graph.
- Test prediction and controlled reconfiguration after member churn, model
  checkpoint replacement, schema migration, fork, merge, and partial damage.
- Compare with world-only model, external self registry, self-only inventory,
  label-preserving snapshot copy, forged-but-consistent ancestry, and
  history-free model swap.

Pass only if self-inclusive state improves prediction or action under controlled
self-change and identity-bearing dispositions, permission boundaries,
provenance, retention, and remove/restore effects survive generation change.
This is functional self-model evidence only, never consciousness or personhood.
Claim ceiling: `BOUNDED_WORLD_SELF_CONTINUITY`.

A G4 precursor is a separate `PRECURSOR`-mode experiment requiring valid G0 and
G1 decisions. It may test only a bounded fixed-membership world/self,
lineage, permission, or retention component against world-only, external
registry, snapshot, and forged-lineage nulls. Before G3 closes it cannot count
as topology morphogenesis, integrated identity, or composition evidence; it
sets `unlock_authorized = false` and has ceiling
`WORLD_SELF_CONTINUITY_COMPONENT_SIGNAL_ONLY`.

### G5 — bounded two-scale HSWM-of-HSWMs

The first compositional test uses at least two independently measured HSWM
candidates and exactly two bounded scales. It does not jump to society or
unbounded fractal closure.

- Every constituent counted as an HSWM must have independently owned PASS
  decisions and evidence UIDs for G1 through integrated G4, including its own
  `Inv`/`Permit`, lineage, credit, consent/exit, rollback, and current capability.
  A weaker worker may appear only in a negative federation/control arm; it
  cannot satisfy FCL-2 or FCL-8 same-type preservation.
- The composite must itself implement the same schema-level
  `Step / Learn / Inv / Permit / lineage` signature.
- Before the run, bind the macro intervention target, pre-treatment fixed micro
  factors, the allowed distribution of micro realizations, forbidden
  divergence, the matched micro intervention family, and alternate macro
  partitions. The primary estimand is the total macro intervention effect;
  post-treatment micro state is measured as a mediator, never conditioned on
  to manufacture a residual. Any direct-effect mediation estimand is secondary
  and separately identified. If this identification contract cannot be met,
  the automatic ceiling is
  `HIERARCHICAL_COORDINATION_WITHOUT_MACRO_CAUSAL_IDENTIFICATION`.
- Apply regrouping, member exit, conflict, latency, model swap, and schema
  migration.
- Compare with stateless wrapper, message bus, federation, flat monolith,
  centralized orchestrator, nested graph without whole-state, alternate macro
  partition, and global-only reward.

Pass only if the total macro intervention effect survives comparison with the
matched micro intervention family and alternate macro partitions, wrapper and
commander explanations are bounded, the whole-level causal rung closes, and
member identity, credit, rights, provenance, consent, exit, and rollback remain
separable. Claim ceiling:
`TWO_SCALE_BOUNDED_CAUSAL_COMPOSITION`; individual, organizational, social, and
unbounded scale closure remain unjudged.

### G6 — replication and scale stress

G6 asks whether G5 survives replication, not whether structural nesting can be
drawn indefinitely.

- Replicate across task families, model families, seeds, evaluator ownership,
  and at least one independent implementation or team.
- Increase only one primary scale stress at a time: member count, coalition
  variety, latency, churn, conflict, privacy restriction, exit rate, schema
  migration, or topology damage.
- Report performance, uncertainty, coordination cost, causal learning yield,
  invariant effectiveness, rights/exit preservation, and recovery.

Pass at one tested range permits only `BOUNDED_REPLICATED_SCALING`. It does not
permit claims of infinite self-similarity, consciousness, selfhood, social
legitimacy, or causal closure at untested scales.

A control family is not a baseline name. It identifies an alternative
explanation, the intervention that distinguishes it, factors that must remain
matched, primary observables, a failure inference, and the claim ceiling imposed
when the explanation remains open. A control definition is never a control run;
an execution must create separate run, instrument, outcome, and decision
artifacts.

| `CF-01` model/compute null | stronger model, sampling, context, or compute caused the gain | same-model and compute-matched direct/single-agent arms | `HSWM_SPECIFIC_UPLIFT_NOT_IDENTIFIED` |

No experiment should vary all fourteen families at once. Each run declares one
primary intervention family and holds all other applicable confound axes fixed.
Factorial or sequential designs require an explicit interaction plan and larger
sample budget.

An apparent HSWM win with an unmatched axis is not a failed HSWM result. It is

## Run-level decision contract

Every run must keep definition, execution, observation, control judgment, and
gate promotion separate.

```text
CONTROL_RUN
ControlFamily definition
→ preregistered ControlInstance and MatchedBudget
→ pre-outcome sealed trajectory and intervention identity
→ independently owned Outcome and instrument record
→ ObservedResult
→ ControlAssessment
→ PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE ClaimDecision (CONTROL scope; never unlocks)

GATE_PROMOTION
recursively validated, SHA-bound CONTROL_RUN source bundles
+ recursively validated prerequisite GateDecision bundles
→ GateAssessment over the exact required-control and prerequisite sets
→ GateDecision (GATE scope)
```

- Control `PASS`: the named alternative explanation was bounded for the
  declared task, intervention, budget, and uncertainty. Its ceiling is only
  `CONTROL_ALTERNATIVE_BOUNDED_WITHIN_SCOPE`; it cannot unlock a gate.
- `FAIL`: the alternative remains viable or directly explains the effect. Lower
  the claim to the control's declared ceiling and return to its predecessor.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: outcome independence, budget matching, instrument validity,
  or intervention identity failed. Its ceiling is
  `INCONCLUSIVE_MEASUREMENT_NOT_READY`; do not treat it as a null mechanism
  effect.
- Gate `PASS`: all required control decisions and prerequisite gate decisions
  are recursively validated, same-subject and same-scope, and the gate pass rule
  is satisfied. Only this layer may authorize the gate's declared unlock.

No `PASS` may be inferred from test count, green CI, KG publication, trace
volume, ontology size, model size, agent count, or topological complexity.

The definition ontology also declares, but does not instantiate, eleven evidence
artifact kinds: `ControlInstance`, `MatchedBudget`, `PreOutcomeSeal`,
`OutcomeSource`, `InstrumentCalibration`, `ObservedResult`, `ControlAssessment`,
`ClaimDecision`, `GateAssessment`, `GateDecision`, and `BoundaryEvidence`.
Executed evidence must
use separate instances and typed bridges; the definitions themselves never
count as a run or result. Conditional extension schemas make CF-08 lifecycle
cost, CF-13 selection integrity, CF-14 authority enforcement, G4 precursor
routing, and G5 macro identification machine-checkable instead of hiding them
behind an opaque protocol hash. The fail-closed run-bundle validator must accept
required fields, recursive source-bundle hashes, UID references,
assessment/decision consistency, exact required-control and prerequisite sets,
ceiling transitions, and conditional extensions before any gate `PASS`. A
counted G5 constituent must resolve to same-subject recursively validated
`GateDecision` records for G1–G4, with G4 in `INTEGRATED` mode; a `PRECURSOR`,
`FAIL`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, foreign-subject, or detached self-attested decision
cannot be substituted.

Two verification boundaries remain explicit. Recursive canonical SHA-256 proves
the structural identity and lineage of the embedded bundle; it does not prove
who authored it or that the asserted outcome owner is independently
authenticated. Therefore external or public gate promotion is blocked until a
signed receipt or independently resolvable source URI is verified against an
authorized owner identity and revocation state. Likewise, `GateAssessment`
fields such as `pass_rule_state` and `evidence_summary_sha256` are currently
reviewer-issued and structurally bound. The validator checks their state
contract, source coverage, scope, extensions, and enclosing bundle, but does not
recompute the scientific conclusion from raw observations. Automatic scientific
promotion requires an executable analysis specification and deterministic
recalculation from immutable outcome artifacts.

Validate an evidence bundle before considering any control or gate decision:

```bash
uv run python scripts/validate_hswm_causal_composition_run.py <bundle.json>
```

## Ragnarok and PIDNA coupling

Ragnarok and PIDNA provide orthogonal metacognitive pressure on this sequence.

- Only a revision passing `CF-05` and the relevant credit controls counts toward
  causal learning yield.
- Static instruction bytes, interpretation work, manual exceptions, evidence
  latency, invalid-run share, and scale overhead are reported beside quality.
- Lifecycle cost is partitioned into setup, adaptation, per-task operation,
  maintenance, and evaluation, with the declared amortization horizon and both
  successful and failed runs retained in the denominator.
- Constitutional permission, consent, rollback, and safety costs are separated
  from ordinary case-specific routing burden; removing rights to win is invalid.
- A PIDNA rung requires independent outcome, causal credit, admitted revision,
  and changed next behavior. A receipt without mediation is not ascent.
- If verification artifacts and static exception work grow faster than valid
  causal revisions, the program records an anti-Ragnarok failure instead of
  adding more judgment machinery.

qualification. It should use one narrow persistent environment, one revision
kind, one admission path, and the complete `CF-01`, `CF-02`, `CF-05`, `CF-06`,

G2–G6 may be designed and simulated, but no downstream success label is
authorized before G1 closes.
