## 4. What is actually unexpected

The episode does not manufacture an anomaly from a missing value. It starts
from three already bounded observations:

1. Strict full-rate-good horizontal affine scalar tails contract to one reduced
   relative class, but uniform goodness does not select its phase-locked
   representative.
2. The literal Lorentzian source arms lie on the oscillatory boundary of that
   good band. Extending them through a complex cap is a candidate lift, not a
   source-selected physical cycle.
3. At the fold, both tracked local laterals remain viable. The shortcut
   `origin lateral + relative determinant transport → unique outgoing fold arm`
   is locally contradicted.

The nonzero-lapse scalar source-link control later retained a bounded scalar
link, but it did not choose the phase-locked representative, close the
zero-lapse distribution, construct the physical joint cycle, or determine a
global coefficient.

The causal signature for proposal search is therefore:

```text
local pieces remain admissible
+ one reduced local class exists
+ source boundary is known only in a narrower scope
+ more than one fold continuation survives
→ global source-to-fold selection is not identified
```

The next question must test this local-to-global seam. Another backend, dtype,
tolerance sweep, local root fit, or unrelated ontology edge does not.

## 5. Frozen competing mechanism families

V0 freezes three mechanism families rather than asking an LLM to invent and
judge its own population. They partition the declared finite-atlas outcomes;
they do not claim to exhaust the full theory.

### M-A — source-selected unique lift

The declared source (`iε`)/origin prescription extends through all
overlaps as exactly one admissible relative-cycle lateral.

- Prediction: independent path decompositions reach the same relative-basis
  vector and only one lateral satisfies every frozen source, orientation,
  good-end, and joint-admissibility condition.
- Kill: two source-compatible laterals survive with a stable nontrivial basis
  jump, or no candidate survives.

### M-B — Stokes/lateral split

At least two source-compatible continuations survive, and their difference is
a nonzero, same-typed relative-cycle basis jump under the frozen patch system.

- Prediction: both laterals pass source and joint-admissibility checks while
  the independently reconstructed overlap transports differ by the same stable
  nonidentity transition.
- Kill: exactly one survives, the difference vanishes under an admissible
  convention change, or no candidate survives.

### M-C — joint-lift obstruction

The scalar affine tail can be good in isolation but cannot extend through the
declared source, field, gauge, and BFV eligibility conditions as an admissible
joint lift.

- Prediction: an exhaustive declared candidate set hits a proved bad end,
  divisor, orientation incompatibility, or joint-admissibility obstruction.
- Kill: one or more candidates complete every frozen overlap and eligibility
  check.

Failure to construct a complete candidate set, same-typed transition, or valid
independent check yields `INCONCLUSIVE`; it is not evidence for M-C.

lineage-disjoint episodes whose episode-specific outcome-to-question mappings

randomization unit, blocking and interference rules, sample-size and power

#### Lock C — split the two outcome-custody chains

The future protocol must create two separately keyed, sealed, and audited
bindings:

1. **Revision-input custody:** Custodian A holds the ICE atlas verdict;
   Evaluators A1 and A2 judge it before scoped credit is assigned.
2. **Endpoint custody:** a distinct Custodian B holds each fresh selected-probe
   outcome; arm-blind Evaluators B1 and B2 judge Discriminator Yield@1 without
   access to arm identity or revision bytes.

The two chains must use lineage-disjoint cases, keys, accounts, manifests, and
reveal events. The mathematical cover/type reviewer decides design
admissibility and may not substitute for either custodian or evaluator. Leakage
or identity overlap between the chains makes the occurrence `VOID`.

#### Loss-aware hyperedge projection

An internal n-ary hyperedge has one UID, and each role-bearing participant
incidence has its own UID, version, owner reference, role, position,
multiplicity, qualifiers, provenance, and temporal/outcome scope. The bounded
projections preserve those identities as follows:

```text
HSWM internal:
  Hyperedge(e_uid,
    participants[(incidence_uid, atom_uid, role, position, multiplicity), ...])

RDF 1.1 view:
  one IRI resource for e_uid
  + one role-qualified incidence resource per participant
  + one named graph per sealed projection phase

GQL / SQL-PGQ view:
  (:Hyperedge {uid, version, snapshot_digest})
    -[:PARTICIPANT {
        incidence_uid, hyperedge_uid, version, role, position, multiplicity
      }]->(:Atom {uid})
```

Pairwise clique expansion is prohibited as the authoritative projection because
it invents pairwise relations and loses the all-participants-together
constraint, role, order, multiplicity, and invertibility. A convenience
pairwise view must be marked `DERIVED_NON_INVERTIBLE`, retain
`source_hyperedge_uids[]` and the complete source-incidence list, bind its
derivation rule and snapshot digest, publish a loss profile, and remain
ineligible for re-ingestion. Multiple origins may not be silently coalesced.

Every projection manifest must bind:

```text
projection_id
canonical_snapshot_digest
schema_version and episode_uid
source revision and projection time
standard versions and corrigenda
mapping_spec_version
exact read/query capability allowlist
mapping_loss flags
inverse_mapping = false by default
exporter version, commit, digest and license
```

At minimum, `mapping_loss` states whether n-ary atomicity is reified, and
whether hyperedge and incidence identity, role, position, multiplicity,
qualifiers, temporal/outcome scope, provenance, owner/authorization references,
and reverse reconstruction are preserved. `inverse_mapping = true` is allowed
only when every projected participant retains immutable incidence and source
hyperedge UIDs, version, all declared qualifiers and scopes, the mapping is
injective within the frozen schema, position/cardinality uniqueness rules are
declared, and an exact reconstruction test passes.

#### Graph-delta invention classifier

For a proposed change `delta_G`, reviewers compare the before/after graph
projection and the authority-bearing source records:

```text
INVENTION_CARRYING_CANDIDATE iff delta_G changes at least one of:
  P = a mechanism's risky prediction or kill condition
  M = the identity or scope of a live competing mechanism family
  Q(y) = the next executable discriminator selected because of sealed outcome y

SUPPORTING_INFRASTRUCTURE iff delta_G only makes an already selected
  discriminator measurable, attributable, reproducible, or portable

MAINTENANCE otherwise
```

Changing an edge label, graph layout, embedding, rank, backend, serialization,
shape, signature, package, or dashboard is not invention unless the exact
`P`, `M`, or outcome-bound `Q(y)` delta is also identified. Even then the label
is `INVENTION_CARRYING_CANDIDATE`, not a scientific result, until the relevant
sealed outcome and independent judgment exist.

## 10. Terminals, claim ceilings, and stopping

### One-episode calibration acceptance

Before Custodian A reveals the atlas verdict, a future protocol must compile
each section 7 branch to exact selected-question bytes and seal their hashes.
`TRANSITION_SEMANTICS_CALIBRATED` is issued only when all of the following hold:

1. every Lock-A transition invariant and the exact pre-state/post-state binding
   passes;
2. both Lock-C custody chains pass, A1/A2 agree on the revision-input verdict,
   and arm-blind B1/B2 agree on the selected-probe endpoint;
3. the compiled `INV_H1_OUTCOME_BOUND` selected-question hash equals the frozen
   section 7 oracle hash for the sealed verdict;
4. that selected question differs from `INV_C0_NO_UPDATE` in at least one exact
   risky prediction, live-rival separation, or executable discriminator; and
5. `INV_I1_REMOVE` eliminates the attributed selection change and
   `INV_I2_RESTORE` byte-identically restores both the revision and its compiled
   selected-question behavior.

A valid measurement that misses items 3–5 terminates as
`CALIBRATION_TRANSITION_FAILED`. A malformed owner-valid transition or failed
state/Permit invariant terminates as `CALIBRATION_TRANSITION_INVALID`. Custody,
seal, evaluator-independence, or leakage failure terminates as `VOID`. None of
these terminals authorizes an automatic retry.

## 11. Invention-versus-maintenance rule

For this programme, a proposed document, edge, adapter, or calculation is
`INVENTION_CARRYING` only if a reviewer can point to at least one exact change
in:

1. a mechanism's risky prediction;
2. the set or scope of a live rival mechanism; or
3. the next discriminating experiment selected because of an outcome.

An artifact that changes none of these is `MAINTENANCE`, or
`SUPPORTING_INFRASTRUCTURE` when it is required to make an already named
discriminator valid. Neither category counts as scientific invention or HSWM
progress.

No new ontology, bridge, ranking service, agent role, or execution framework is
authorized until this finite discriminator has a named mathematical reviewer,
a complete same-typed patch/overlap specification, and a frozen acceptance
table. If those cannot be supplied, stop this episode rather than adding
machinery.
