# PIDNA research project

Status: **HISTORICAL_CONCEPT_RECOVERED_DESIGN_SEEDED_UNJUDGED**.

## What PIDNA means

The user-corrected name is **PIDNA — Pure Intelligence DNA**. The historical
LakatoTree texts `TOUCH_THE_SKY.md` and `docs/PIDNA.md` define it as the structure
of intellectual progress: a blue strand of conjecture, abstraction, prediction,
and imagination winds around a red strand of execution, external measurement,
and refutation. A paired conjecture and verification forms a rung; repeated
pairing produces an ascending helix.

The durable insight is not the DNA shape by itself:

> A bold conjecture may launch work, but ascent is not real until external
> outcome closes the loop and changes what the cognitive system does next.

The historical source is user-owned retired material, read from a local archive
on 2026-08-29. It is not copied back into this repository. Its conceptual
content is research input; its deleted governance services, ledgers, gates,
canonical-write paths, and product topology remain retired.

## Target identity, current evidence, and conceptual delta

| layer | statement |
|---|---|
| historical target | PIDNA pairs conjecture and verification so intellectual progress cannot be self-reported. |
| current HSWM target | A proposed trajectory becomes ascent only through an independently grounded outcome, multiscale causal credit, owner-valid canonical revision, and a measurable change in later behavior. |
| current evidence | The historical Lean kernel proves properties of its hand-written formal model. It does not prove the Python system, the honesty of the external `novel` input, HSWM learning, or empirical superiority. |
| conceptual delta | PIDNA is reopened as a temporal hypergraph recurrence across HSWM scales, not restored as a fixed two-service architecture or treated as proof that a tree is geometrically a helix. |

### Translation into the current HSWM ontology

The two strands are **roles in a provenance-bound transition**, not permanent
subsystems or fixed owners.

- Blue: one or more schema-admissible conjecture/proposal atoms and the
  trajectories they initiate.
- Red: independently derived observations, interventions, measurements, and
  falsifiers, including explicit instrument uncertainty.
- Rung: an n-ary outcome-binding hyperedge joining proposal, trajectory,
  outcome, causal credit, admitted canonical revision, and later behavioral
  effect.
- Ascent: a sequence of valid rungs that increases prospective problem-solving
  capacity under fresh, retention, safety, and removal controls.

The historical one-prediction/one-receipt picture is a useful minimum case, not
a mandatory 1:1 data model. Real HSWM outcomes may credit several cells and
relations; one proposal may produce several delayed outcomes. PIDNA therefore
requires typed n-ary incidence and explicit ambiguity rather than a forced pair.

## Fractal reopening

The historical PIDNA document rejected the literal claim that an entire branch
tree is a larger geometric helix. That rejection stands. A tree/DAG and a helix
are different shapes.

The current HSWM-of-HSWMs direction reopens a different, stronger question:
**does the same causal recurrence close at several cognitive scales?** A cell,
agent, organization, or bounded composite can each instantiate:

```text
conjecture → action/trajectory → external outcome → multiscale credit
→ canonical revision → changed next behavior
```

This is functional self-similarity of the ignition law, not pictorial identity.
If the recurrence survives composition while member identity, credit, rights,
and lineage remain separable, PIDNA may describe a fractal research dynamic. If
the higher scale is only aggregation or a central judge, the hypothesis fails.

The proposed recurrence maps to FCL-1 local causal learning, FCL-2 composition
preservation, FCL-3 emergent coalition, FCL-4 multiscale credit, FCL-5 topology
morphogenesis, FCL-7 diachronic continuity, and FCL-8 HSWM-of-HSWMs. FCL-6
world-self co-modeling is not established or tested by the current PIDNA
sequence. The mapping is a set of falsifiable bridge hypotheses, not evidence
that PIDNA already realizes any law.

## Relationship to Ragnarok

PIDNA and Ragnarok constrain each other.

- Blue without red becomes fluent conjecture, confabulation, or ungrounded
  architecture.
- Red without blue becomes measurement without a content-increasing hypothesis.
- Red implemented as ever-growing static gates and judgment documents becomes
  Ragnarok even if every gate is deterministic.
- A healthy PIDNA cycle must improve **causal learning yield per verification
  burden**, not merely accumulate more receipts.

Thus the red strand is not infallible. Instruments can be blind, metrics can be
gamed, and a deterministic judge can deterministically process false inputs.
Independent negative controls, source ownership, calibration, and changed-next-
behavior tests are part of the research object.

## Research questions

1. What is the minimal typed n-ary PIDNA rung for HSWM without collapsing
   proposal, evidence, credit, revision, and permission into one score?
2. Does paired conjecture-verification outperform conjecture-only and
   verification-only arms under equal budgets?
3. Does a rung cause a canonical revision and changed held-out behavior, or only
   create a log entry?
4. How should delayed and shared outcomes allocate credit across cells,
   coalitions, and hyperedges?
5. When should PIDNA branches remain separate, merge, or be retired without
   destroying contributor lineage?
6. Is the recurrence invariant under model swap and bounded HSWM-of-HSWMs
   composition?
7. Can verification burden remain bounded enough to avoid Ragnarok?

## Hypotheses and falsifiers

### PID-H1 — paired-cycle advantage

The paired PIDNA arm will produce more fresh-task, causally validated revisions
per equal resource budget than blue-only conjecture generation, red-only testing,
and unpaired log baselines.

Reject it if the paired arm has no preregistered gain, if any gain is explained
by additional calls or human work, or if verification latency erases it.

### PID-H2 — behavioral rung

A valid rung changes the next HSWM transition distribution through an admitted
canonical revision.

Reject it if outcome or verdict intervention does not change later behavior, or
if removing the revision preserves the claimed effect.

### PID-H3 — hypergraph credit

Typed n-ary proposal/trajectory/outcome/credit incidence will preserve shared
and delayed credit more accurately than forced 1:1 pairs or flat logs.

Reject it if calibration, counterfactual attribution, or replay is no better
under matched information and compute.

### PID-H4 — functional scale recurrence

The same causal recurrence will close at component and bounded composite scales
while preserving member lineage, regrouping behavior, and intervention effects.

Reject it if the higher-scale result requires a fixed central orchestrator, loses
member credit, or cannot survive regrouping and model swap.

### PID-H5 — anti-Ragnarok verification

As valid rungs accumulate, verification burden per causally validated revision
will remain bounded or decrease without weakening invariant effectiveness.

Reject it if static gates, exception rules, or evidence latency grow faster than
causal learning yield.

## Experiment sequence

1. **PID-E0 — historical formal audit.** Map every theorem in the retired
   `formal/Pidna.lean` kernel to the prose claim it actually establishes and to
   the unproved boundary input it assumes. Output is a semantic audit, not HSWM
   evidence.
2. **PID-E1 — minimal causal rung.** Build the smallest current-schema episode
   from prospective conjecture through external outcome, causal revision, next-
   behavior change, and remove/restore. Do not restore LakatoTree services.
3. **PID-E2 — three-arm necessity trial.** Compare blue-only, red-only, and
   paired PIDNA arms with equal task, model, token, tool, wall-time, and human
   budgets.
4. **PID-E3 — n-ary branch/merge trial.** Introduce shared outcomes,
   contradictory evidence, delayed credit, fork, merge, and replay; test
   confluence and lineage.
5. **PID-E4 — HSWM-of-HSWMs recurrence.** Compose already measured units and
   test the same recurrence under regrouping, model swap, and bounded
   interventions.

The first runnable work item is `PID-E0`, followed by the smallest `PID-E1`
mechanism. A formal model or ontology expansion alone cannot satisfy either.
