`ForkAllocation` maps each of the four typed arms to one opaque clone. Lean
requires the four clone identities to be distinct and both proposal and probe
orders to be permutations of the complete four-arm universe.

`GenerationLedger` uses dependent call roles:


The observed call-ID list must equal those nine named calls, call IDs and
