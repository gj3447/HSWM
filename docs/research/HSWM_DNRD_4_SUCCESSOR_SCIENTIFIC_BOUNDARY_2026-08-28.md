## Canonical role

HSWM's target identity remains one token-native LLM-function macro-neural
network whose evolving hypergraph is at once its living harness, world model,
and continuous learner.  An outcome-bound, schema-admitted canonical revision
must be able to condition a later token-native traversal or transition for
that target to have the claimed learning role.

DNRD-4 is not that target and is not a separate router, cognition system,
learner, or world model.  It is a bounded projection of one proposed durable
routing-disposition transition.  Its local experimental atoms have one
schema-relative responsibility owner, typed state/content/journal references,
and provenance-bound outcome records.  The repository ontology, bridge,
ledgers, CI, public pulse, and judge are evidence interfaces, not HSWM
cognition or learning.  Passing their tests or completing this finite
diagnostic cannot by itself constitute HSWM progress.

Even a complete GO is therefore an instrument result: it can show that one
deterministic routing-state implementation and its evidence path conform to
their finite contract.  It cannot be scientific effect evidence for an LLM or
HSWM learning mechanism.

## Exact bounded conformance question and construction

> In one source/runtime-trusted finite engineering-conformance occurrence with
> repeated-context exhaustive forced exposure,
> does a response-independent scorer-outcome-bound integer routing payload
> persist across fresh-process recovery and alter pre-model route selection
> relative to exact W0 rollback and context-binding derangement, while
> fixed-rule replay of the same retained training update records reproduces W1
> without model dispatch?

The construction remains intentionally finite and deterministic:

- Four new future-pulse-seeded streams each contain four context keys and two
  routes.  Training forces every context-by-route cell once: eight training
  calls per stream and 32 total.
- Heldout evaluation reuses the same four contexts.  It is not unseen-context
  generalization, independent utility evaluation, or a statistical discovery
  study.
- `FULL` applies eight locally declared, response-independent scorer outcomes
  per stream to the durable integer routing payload.
- `NO_MEMORY_ROLLBACK` recovers the exact immutable W0 genesis payload with no
  learned update.
- `BINDING_DERANGED_NUMERIC_PLACEBO` uses a within-stratum fixed-point-free
  permutation of context bindings while preserving numeric payload byte count,
  precision, update multiset, and L1/L2 norms.  It does not claim full atom,
  history, or reference parity.
- Fixed-rule replay is a no-model admission gate, not a fourth model arm: from
  W0, the same retained FULL update records and fixed integer rule must
  reproduce W1's payload and pre-model route readouts.
- There are eight heldout probes per stream per arm: 4 streams × 3 arms × 8
  probes = 96 evaluation calls, hence at most 128 generation calls in all.
  There are no retries, provider-cache independence claims, or substitution of
  a failed call.

Before the new seed is known, the finite rule requires every stream to have:
`FULL` positive route reward on 8/8 probes; W0 positive on 4/8;
`DERANGED` positive on at most 4/8; `FULL` different from W0 on exactly 4/8;
and `FULL` different from `DERANGED` on at least 4/8.  Recovery, exact
rollback/restore, fixed-rule replay, parity, leakage, chronology, call
accounting, and complete evidence closure are equally mandatory.  A complete,
valid occurrence failing that rule is `DIAGNOSTIC_NO_GO`; an evidence or
identity contradiction is `VOID_PROTOCOL`; and a sealed bounded operational
failure is `INCONCLUSIVE_OCCURRENCE`.

The model remains a format/liveness boundary that is presented selected
evidence, not the learner under test.  The scorer outcome is declared
response-independent, but its isolation is not proven.  Positive separation
is substantially construction-driven.

More strongly, the admitted model response need only be one of the two public
candidate tokens; it need not match the evidence selected by the route.  A
dummy boundary returning any schema-valid candidate can therefore leave the
state update, route-reward counts, and GO rule unchanged.  The four streams are
future-seeded finite label partitions, not independent sampling units, and no
population estimand or error model is defined.  DNRD-4 can falsify an
implementation-conformance claim, but it cannot estimate or confirm a causal
LLM/HSWM effect.

## Source-A instrument-readiness gates

No DNRD-4 generation is authorized until a new Source A implements and CI
verifies all of the following against the production-shaped closure.  These
requirements are pre-observation engineering gates, not a modification of
DNRD-3 evidence.

1. **Semantic JSON and byte provenance.** Retain each raw accepted response
   body and separately retain the canonical digest of its parsed, one-property
   schema-valid object.  Admission validates semantic schema, enum membership,
   and required `finish_reason`; it must not equate server whitespace with the
   canonical serialization.  Qualification must exercise valid pretty and
   compact JSON and reject malformed, extra-key, and out-of-enum bodies.  Its
   bounded receipt pins the exact `qualify`/`live`/`runner`/`task_family`
   Source-A closure and the frozen Python/Unicode runtime both before and after
   the three calls; register, executor, and judge independently join those
   identities to the source manifest and preregistration runtime bindings.
2. **Manifest-authorized empty regular files.** Every manifest row declares a
   safe path, `bytes` length, and SHA-256; the enclosing closure contract
   requires a regular file and fixes the sealed copy's mode.  The shared
   producer/judge rule permits `bytes: 0` only when the retained file is empty
   and its hash equals the declared hash (including the empty-byte hash).  It
   rejects a missing, substituted, special, or length/hash-mismatched file.  The actual
   `@standard-schema/spec/dist/index.js` zero-byte path is exercised, rather
   than abstracted away.
3. **Post-acceptance terminal closure rehearsal.** Any internal failure after
   an accepted live event must seal a complete indexed terminal as
   `VOID_PROTOCOL`, not leave an unclassified partial occurrence.  Before
   freeze, no-network terminal rehearsals exercise three distinct cases: a
   candidate with 128 valid pretty-serialized accepted responses; an
   inconclusive occurrence ending in a retained model-boundary rejection or
   ambiguity; and a post-acceptance internal structural void.  Separately, a
   checkout-faithful closure rehearsal copies and judge-scans the real
   4,050-file selected runtime, including its known zero-byte member, with
   exact path, byte-length, hash, and sealed-mode checks.  The terminal
   fixtures do not splice that runtime into a synthetic Source-A/CI chain,
   because doing so would invent provenance.  Together the rehearsals must
   retain raw bridge-state evidence transactionally before interpretation and
   exercise independent frozen-judge handling of every prescribed terminal
   path; the live occurrence must still bind one coherent source/runtime/CI
   chain before any scientific judgment is possible.

The new source manifest, runtime closure, bridge/scorer/verifier identities,
and CI evidence must be freshly generated.  DNRD-3's runtime receipt and
closure cannot satisfy these gates.

Source A therefore carries four bounded operator tools as part of the frozen
evidence interface.  The offline preparer creates the exact source/runtime
manifests and content-addressed CI receipts from already obtained bytes; the
qualification tool makes exactly three disjoint, seed-free, no-retry model
calls and retains only its bounded operational summary; and the registration
builder emits the sole canonical preregistration-B blob from the frozen pins.
After successful B CI, the configuration builder proves the one-path A-to-B
transition, cross-validates the retained receipts and runtime identities, and
creates only fresh owner-only mutable roots plus one canonical execution
configuration outside the checkout.  The preparer, registration builder, and
configuration builder do not contact a model or network, and none of these
tools creates an experiment seed, result, judgment, canonical admission, or
HSWM learning transition.

## Promotion boundary and later question

The strongest possible DNRD-4 GO remains
`DIAGNOSTIC_INTEGRITY_GO_NO_UTILITY_CLAIM`.  It may only justify a separately
preregistered successor design and is classified as engineering conformance,
not scientific effect evidence.  It never establishes response-caused outcome
or update, independent sampling, a population estimate, LLM learning,
unseen-context generalization, independent outcome/scorer isolation, utility,
durable-state superiority, HSWM continuous-learning efficacy, topology
learning, role specialization, transfer, consolidation, canonical admission,
or Permit.

`COUNTERFACTUAL_CREDIT_REVERSAL` is intentionally not added as a fourth DNRD-4
model arm.  It would test whether equal-magnitude retained records with an
inverted outcome-to-credit binding induce the preregistered complementary route
pattern, but by itself would not remove the construction-driven nature of this diagnostic.
It would increase live calls from 128 to 160 and add new mount, parity, and
judge complexity.  It is permitted only as a separately frozen DNRD-5
scientific question after DNRD-4 has an independently judged, complete,
non-void terminal.  A DNRD-4 `VOID_PROTOCOL` or `INCONCLUSIVE_OCCURRENCE` is
not a license to add it or to retry DNRD-4.

A scientifically stronger DNRD-5 must additionally make the sealed model
response causally relevant to an independently attributable external outcome,
bind that outcome to credit before the update, measure the changed later
token/action under reversal and non-reversal controls, and preregister its
sampling unit, estimand, error rule, missingness/publication handling, and
public pre-pulse commitment.  Merely adding another deterministic routing
projection would still be harness growth rather than evidence for HSWM's
continuous-learning loop.
