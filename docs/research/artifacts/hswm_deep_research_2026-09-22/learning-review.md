# Natural-language relation revision and world-model hypothesis learning

2026-09-22 · `SECONDARY_AI_LITERATURE_MEMO` · `NO_HSWM_EFFICACY_OR_CR_FCL_PROMOTION`

## Decision

The import is a bounded candidate-revision experiment, not generic
“self-correction”: bind a proposed semantic delta to an exact RelationVersion,
an independently observed outcome, evaluator, candidate set, and held-out
decision test. Persist only a predeclared improvement and a reproducible
fresh-case behavioural difference over evidence-only and sham controls.

`semanticText` and a declared disposition can thereby be editable research
variables. This does not make them world truth, make an LLM critique causal
credit, or establish HSWM learning. The 2026-09-21 starting negative remains:
four committed revisions changed no semantic fields and oracle local execution
was 27/48. Upstream W1 failure cannot be rescued by scale or topology.

## Prioritized HSWM proposal

1. **W1: oracle local execution first.** Freeze a small relation family and
   prove whether a fresh local LLM execution can use canonical relation text,
   ordered typed roles, context and exceptions to make the required decision.
   Predeclare oracle/outcome, read frame, success threshold, sham/evidence-only
   controls, and cost. The failed 27/48 arm remains a RED/underdetermined
   finding until its stated mediator is resolved.
2. **W2: revision mediation after W1.** W2 schema and evaluator design may run
   alongside W1, but no efficacy result begins until W1 passes. Each candidate
   states a field-level delta (`semanticText`, declared disposition,
   role/exception, evidence-only, no-op); empty semantic deltas cannot be
   called semantic learning.
3. **Candidate selection and test.** Record parent/proposal versions, evaluator
   trace and score. Select predeclared candidates on calibration/selection data,
   then evaluate once on fresh held-out cases with no-op, evidence-only, sham,
   remove/restore and fixed-relation controls. Record candidate recall including
   `other/expand`.
4. **Advance criterion.** Only a changed relation read in fresh local execution
   that changes held-out decision/outcome against equal-cost controls advances.
   Otherwise mark its exact mediator `RED_WITHIN_SCOPE` or `UNDERDETERMINED`,
   retain lineage, and reroute; no topology/batching/multiscale rescue.

## Claim ceiling

These sources suggest proposal, hypothesis-ranking, diagnostics and evaluation
designs only. They discharge no CR-0..7 or FCL-1..8: scores do not establish
causal credit, composition does not establish joint/multiscale credit, and
factor/incidence form does not prove meaningful local execution. Hyperon
remains the mandatory persistent-metagraph/neural-bridge comparator at its
pinned audit maturity; these sources establish no novelty over it.
