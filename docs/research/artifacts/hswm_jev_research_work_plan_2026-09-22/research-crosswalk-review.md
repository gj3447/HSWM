## Specific JEV principles

- **P2 calibration:** retain `CalibrationArtifact` as a split-bound analysis object:
  fit population, relation/model/decision-contract version, method, rejection
  handling and applicability invalidation. It follows R1; it has no canonical
  revision authority and no causal-credit role. Do not reuse a final outcome
  test to choose temperature or a revision.
- **P3 scheduling:** first make a static dependency graph from exact read and
  write sets. Parallelize only reads declared independent of prior writes;
  schedule dependent reads after their predecessor transition. This is an
  engineering feasibility task, not shared-trunk computation evidence. Its
  performance and semantic-equivalence test belongs in R4.
- **P4 candidates/expand:** model candidate omission explicitly. A high score
  conditional on a candidate list says nothing about an absent answer. The
  `other/expand` request needs an observable source, budget, provenance and
  predeclared selection/stop rule; candidate proposal is distinct from topology
  admission and from discovering a grounded relation.
- **P6 recursion:** do not begin with arbitrary nesting. First bind each
  boundary to the same outcome-to-revision and read-back execution trace, then
  test the lossy parent abstraction. Persistence of a provenance DAG alone is
  not world/self prediction or composition closure.

## Missing protocol tasks and non-negotiable assumptions

1. Declare the evaluation population/world unit, outcome observer, assignment
   probability, interference boundary, missing-outcome rule and serving-repeat
   aggregation. Temperature/seed do not establish deterministic execution.
2. Separate selection, calibration, final held-out test and post-test future
   data. Final-test feedback retires that test from confirmatory reuse.
3. Version the full relation-incidence payload, read payload, candidate-set
   universe, parser and evaluator; prove round-trip restoration of these bytes
   without treating bitwise model-output replay as required.
4. Predeclare W3 sufficiency horizons and control/learning questions, and W4
   joint-law metric/factorization null. A full read must exclude hidden labels.
5. Define W5’s world and self observables, recovery/exit law, intervention
   mapping and error/retention metrics before seeing scale results. No FCL cell
   or consciousness claim follows from an implementation hierarchy.

   separate admission/permission decision and provenance of each evaluator.
